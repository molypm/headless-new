Here are the questions we get asked most, grouped by topic.

Headless & Migration
What is headless WordPress, and how is it different from my current site?
Headless separates your content (still managed in WordPress, same dashboard your team already knows) from how visitors actually experience the site. That front-end is rebuilt to be fast and flexible, without touching how you publish content day to day.
Do I need to rebuild my whole website from scratch?
No. Your existing content, structure and WordPress dashboard stay as they are. We rebuild the front end your visitors see, your team keeps publishing exactly as before.
Will going headless affect my SEO rankings?
Done properly, it should help rather than hurt. Page speed is a ranking factor, and headless sites typically load significantly faster, which is also directly tied to conversion, not just search rankings.
How long does a typical headless migration take?

AI Solutions
What exactly is the AI Sales Assistant? Is it just a chatbot?
It holds a real conversation rather than reading from a script. It surfaces the visitor's problem, backs up claims with facts, handles objections honestly, and hands off to your team on WhatsApp when a visitor needs a person, in English, Malay or Chinese today.
Can the WhatsApp AI Assistant work with my existing WhatsApp number?
Yes, it's connected via the official WhatsApp Business API and configured around your specific workflow, answering questions, taking bookings, giving updates, tied to a real, returning customer's number so it can recognise them.
What's the difference between the AI Sales Assistant and the WhatsApp AI Assistant?
The Sales Assistant lives on your website and talks to anonymous visitors. The WhatsApp Assistant runs on your business's own number and recognises real, returning customers, so it can take actions like bookings, not just answer questions.
Do I need a separate app for iOS and Android for the App-Like Experience?
No. It's one build that installs like an app on both platforms, visitors save it to their home screen and you can send push notifications, without the cost or maintenance of separate native apps.
Pricing, Audit & Support
How does the Free Audit work, and is the score accurate?
It runs your URL through Google's own Lighthouse data and shows the median of several runs, since a single test can vary by a few points, that's normal, documented behaviour, not an error. We also show lab data (this test) alongside field data (your real visitors), since they answer slightly different questions.
Which pricing plan is right for my business?
It depends on which add-ons solve a problem you actually have today. See the full plan comparison on our pricing page, or ask us directly and we'll recommend the right fit.
What kind of support do I get after launch?
An in-house team, not outsourced subcontractors. Every plan's monthly fee covers hosting, maintenance and support, so the people who built your site are the same people answering your questions afterward.
Is my data and content safe if I move to a headless setup?
Generally, yes, more so than a traditional setup. Your WordPress backend isn't directly exposed to the public in a headless architecture, which means fewer attack vectors and less brand risk.
