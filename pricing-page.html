PRICING FAQ

Pricing questions, answered.

What does the one-time setup cover?

Building and configuring your lightning-fast website and the add-ons in your plan, so it is ready to go live. You can transform your existing site or start fresh.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, maintenance and support, so your site stays fast, secure and up to date, with no surprise costs later.

Which plan should I choose?

Standard suits new sites and small businesses. Premium suits businesses chasing more leads. Corporate suits established businesses ready to scale. Enterprise suits larger teams with specific needs. Not sure? Our free website audit shows where you stand, and we will recommend a plan.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Add-ons can be adjusted as your business grows. Talk to us if a different mix fits you better.
