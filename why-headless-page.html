FAQ

Straight answers before you commit.

What is a lightning-fast website? Is it the same as headless?

Yes. "Headless" or "decoupled" is the technical name. We separate where you manage content from the pages visitors see, and serve those pages ready-made. Same content, far faster.

What do "headless" and "decoupled" mean?

Headless means the part visitors see (the "head") is separated from the back end where content is stored. Decoupled means those two parts are independent and talk through an API. Together they let the front end be fast, secure and easy to extend.

Do I need to change my website platform?

No. Your dashboard, where your team edits the website, stays exactly where it is.

Is this a custom-built system?

No. We don't replace your website with a custom-built system. We keep the platform you already use, like WordPress, Magento or Drupal, and add a fast front end in front of it. You get the speed without moving your content anywhere.

Will my team have to change how they work or need training?

No. Editing, publishing and user roles stay exactly the same.

Which platforms do you support?

WordPress, WooCommerce, Magento, Drupal and Kentico. Using something else? Tell us what you run and we'll discuss it first.

Will it affect my Google rankings?

A faster site helps. We carry over your URLs, titles and redirects so existing rankings are protected.

What does it cost?

From RM3,000 setup plus a monthly fee. See the plans or ask on WhatsApp.
